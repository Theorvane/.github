# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a respectful, constructive open-source community.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Focus criticism on ideas, code, and evidence—not people.
- Welcome different experiences and perspectives.
- Keep technical discussions clear, specific, and in scope.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, doxxing, or deliberate disruption are not acceptable.

## Reporting

Report concerns privately to the organization maintainers through GitHub's private reporting mechanisms where available. Do not escalate a conduct concern through public Issue comments.
